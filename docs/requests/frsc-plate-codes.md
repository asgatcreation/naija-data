# Request to FRSC: list of Local Government plate codes

**How to send:** email it to FRSC's official contact address (shown on frsc.gov.ng under "Contact
Us"), or submit it as a Freedom of Information request, which public institutions must answer
within 7 days under section 4 of the Freedom of Information Act 2011. Keep a copy of what you send
and any reply, and we will record the reply as a source.

---

**To:** The Corps Marshal, Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC), Abuja
**Subject:** Request for the list of Local Government Area codes used on vehicle number plates

Dear Sir/Madam,

I maintain **naija-data** (https://github.com/asgatcreation/naija-data), a free, open-source dataset
of Nigerian reference data used by software developers. It records the official source of every
item it publishes.

Regulation 39(4) of the National Road Traffic Regulations 2012 provides that Local Government Area
codes come first on private, commercial and Local Government vehicle number plates, and regulation
39(5) provides that the Commission determines these codes. I have not been able to find the current
list of codes published by FRSC.

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2011, I respectfully request:

1. The current list of three-letter Local Government Area codes used on vehicle number plates,
   with the Local Government Area and State each code belongs to.
2. Where available, the codes used for Federal and State Government, Armed Forces, Para-military
   and diplomatic plates, and the formats of those plates.
3. The date the list was last updated.

An electronic copy (spreadsheet, CSV or PDF) would be ideal. With your permission, the list will be
published with full credit to FRSC so that software across Nigeria validates plate numbers
correctly.

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours faithfully,

Akanji Oluwaseun Gabriel
asgatcreation@gmail.com
